Behind the apron

Made by Mel,
with intention.

I believe a cake can feel refined without feeling distant. My work is inspired by French pâtisserie, old family tables and the small irregularities that reveal the maker’s hand.

Every element is considered—from the balance of flavour to the last brushstroke of glaze—so what arrives at your table feels completely your own.
