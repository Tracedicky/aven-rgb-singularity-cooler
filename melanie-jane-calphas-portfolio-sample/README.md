# Melanie Jane Calphas — The Art of Imperfection

A review sample of Melanie Jane Calphas's employer-focused pastry portfolio.

## Experience design

- A full-screen title page introduces Melanie and the brand.
- A menu opens three softly fading views: Title, Biography and Gallery.
- The biography presents her self-taught route as evidence of initiative, persistence, creative thinking and coachability.
- The gallery combines finished pieces, texture close-ups and two short process films.
- The existing sales site remains untouched while this sample is reviewed.

## Positioning

Melanie is described accurately as a self-taught, self-directed pastry maker who is ready to bring her practical foundation into a professional team. The copy does not invent employment, qualifications or commercial-kitchen experience.

## Presentation

All bakes are Melanie's own work. Selected backgrounds have been digitally refined with her approval and are disclosed on the gallery page.
