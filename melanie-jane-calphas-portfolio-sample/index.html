Meet Melanie

Resourceful by nature.
Ready for the next kitchen.

Melanie Jane Calphas is a self-taught pastry maker based in Sale, Greater Manchester. She has built her foundation independently through close observation, persistent practice and a willingness to remake things until she understands not only what works, but why.

That route has made her resourceful, curious and comfortable solving problems with her hands. Without being tied to a single way of doing things, she is drawn to unusual flavour combinations, traditional ideas approached differently and the small irregularities that give handmade pastry character.

Self-directed learning is the beginning of her professional story, not a substitute for growth. Melanie is ready to bring that foundation into a supportive working kitchen, where pace, teamwork, feedback and mentorship can take the work further.

“Normal is boring. I do it the Melanie way — imperfection with a hint of madness.”
